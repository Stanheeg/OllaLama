## Do not count the Overall headline as cash yet

The organizer page labels the Overall Winner section as "$12,500 in cash" but immediately describes the actual first-place items as Amazon Echo devices, DevNetwork conference passes and promotion. Until the organizer clarifies that contradiction, TrustFlow's financial plan treats the Overall prize as **unverified/non-cash**.

## Why not force Perfect Corp/name.com into the build

Perfect Corp is consumer/retail AR/AI and name.com is domain operations. Adding either would make the product less coherent and look like sponsor badge-stuffing. The current five-lane design has a single causal workflow and is much easier to defend to judges.
