# 2–4 minute demo script

1. **Problem (15s):** procurement teams research vendors in one tab, read contracts in another, copy results into ticketing systems, and still risk an agent sending a document before a human has verified the evidence.
2. **Create workflow (35s):** enter a vendor and agreement. Show SerpApi evidence and Nutrient-extracted contract facts arriving as separate provenance-bearing inputs.
3. **Agent reasoning (25s):** show deterministic risk score and optional Gemini summary. Point out that web snippets are only review signals, never automatically accepted as facts.
4. **Auditability (25s):** open Xano-backed audit events and show the hash chain across evidence, policy and approval stages.
5. **Human boundary (30s):** attempt document/signature action before approval and show it fail. Approve explicitly.
6. **Document operations (30s):** generate the approval packet with Doctavian. Show structured conditions and evidence summary.
7. **Signing (30s):** create a Foxit eSign draft. Show `sendNow=false`; a human owns the final dispatch/sign step.
8. **Close (15s):** one traceable workflow replaces fragmented search/document/signing work without handing legal authority to the model.
